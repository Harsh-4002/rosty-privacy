1Who we are

Rosty: Night Phone Guard (package com.rostyapp.nightguard) is made by Qissora. Contact: itsharshkantgupta@gmail.com.

There is no account and no login. We run no server that receives your phone data.

2What stays on your phone

Rosty reads, on your phone only, with permissions you grant:

  • Which app is in front and when (Usage Access), to work out your morning grade, pickups and late-night minutes, and to show a pause screen or a lock when you open an app you chose.
  • The time each notification arrived, counted only if you allow Notification access. Titles, text and senders are never read.
  • Your bedtime, wake time, chosen apps, levels, streaks, grade letters, Night Pass records and settings.
  • If you allow notifications, Rosty shows two optional reminders: one in the morning when your grade is ready and one about an hour before bedtime. They are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent to us to make them appear, and you can turn each one off in Settings.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall.

3What leaves your phone

  • Anonymous app events through Google Firebase Analytics, for example "onboarding step viewed" or "pause started". They never contain app names, usage numbers or grades. Firebase also records its own automatic events (first open, sessions, app and OS updates, in-app purchase) with your device model, OS version, app version, country and an app instance ID.
  • Crash reports through Google Firebase Crashlytics: if the app crashes, a report with the technical error trace, your device model, Android version, app version and a random installation ID is sent. We add nothing else to it: no name, no account, no app names, no usage numbers, no grades. Crash reports are sent only from the released app.
  • Rewarded ads through Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one: your advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address and ad interactions.
  • Purchases, reviews and updates are handled by Google Play.

4Your choices

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for consent before ads, analytics or crash reports are used (Google's consent form), and send nothing until you agree. You can change your answer in Settings > Privacy choices for ads. You can reset your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

Rosty has no account, so there is nothing for us to look up or delete on our side: your data about how you use your phone is only on your phone.

5Copies you control

  • Android's backup may keep one small file in your own Google account: levels, streaks, grade letters and a few settings.
  • "Save my progress" writes a similar file where you choose.
  • Neither contains app names, package names, minutes, pickups or notification counts.

6Things you share yourself

The share card and Roast Battle send grade letters (and a nickname you pick) only to the people you choose through the Android share sheet.

7Permissions and why

Usage Access and Display over other apps (warnings and locks), Notification access (optional counts), a foreground service (keeps the warning or lock working while it is on), start at boot (re-arms it after a restart), internet and network state (ads, analytics, purchases), advertising ID (ads), and billing (Pro).

8Children

Rosty is for people 13 and over.

9How long data is kept

On your phone: until you clear storage or uninstall. Firebase Analytics: 2 months. Crashlytics: 90 days. AdMob: according to Google.
